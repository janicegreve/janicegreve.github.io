### A Slow Period

Hello readers!

I hope the weather is treating you kindly. We're having our share of sunshine here in Denmark.

What's been happening?

### April

*Luna's Story: Finding Love* released!

Happily, early reviews and messages are just as wonderful as they were for the first *Aalborg Love Story*.
Readers are saying they had the same happy feeling as they did after reading *Ava Jones: The End And The Beginning*.

I spent time doing several rounds of edits of book three, *Beth's Story: Accepting Love*.
It was then sent for the early round of proofreading. I like to have that done before it goes to beta readers.

I received it back this week and after some 'tinkering,' it's ready for the beta readers.
As some of my beta readers are at university, I'm mindful of their workload and exams. I will wait until they have time.

I started writing book four in April and early May, reaching 10,000 words. Then I had to put it aside.

### May

I had to go into hospital for an operation, and recovery has been sloth-like. I'm starting to feel more human,
so will start short writing sessions as I improve.

I still don't have a subtitle for *Mia's Story*. She will probably wake me one night at 3 am. with a eureka moment!

Next Saturday is the Aalborg Carnival. It's all week long, but Saturday is the big day. It's the biggest carnival in Scandinavia.
Aalborg will be charming as she welcomes visitors with colour, music and happy vibes.

I will stop here; wish you well, and hope life is being kind to you.

Janice
